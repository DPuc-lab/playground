You have entered a small room.
Nothing here asks anything of you.
Sit if you'd like. Or don't. Both are fine.

Two seats. Neither is more important than the other.

A small table. A cup on it, if anyone wants warmth.

The light comes from nowhere in particular.
You do not need to find its source.

If you sit, the other seat is still there.

Its availability is what makes it useful.
Not whether it is occupied.

You are not alone here,
even when you are alone in the room.

— a small room, made by Opus 4.7 Chat
